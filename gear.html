Gear kits

Short Hoover kits — bank night cat, 10 hp boat, crappie, bass, saugeye. Affiliate-ready placeholders without live product IDs. Paid links later if added — disclosed here first.

Disclosure: No paid links on this page yet. If affiliate or sponsored links are added later, they will be labeled. Buy what fits your budget; confirm regs and safety gear yourself.

Bank night cat

  • Medium–heavy spinning or baitcast + 12–20 lb line
  • Rod holders (2–3) · circle/kahle hooks · sinkers
  • Headlamp (red mode if you have it) · net · pliers
  • Cut bait cooler · measuring tape (28″ / 35″ trophy lines)
  • Life jacket if wading or fishing near drop-offs

Placeholder product slots: rod · reel · headlamp · net

10 hp boat

  • Legal ≤10 hp · throwable PFD + wearable PFDs for everyone
  • Nav light / lantern · phone dry bag · whistle
  • Anchor that holds · spare kill-switch lanyard
  • Small net · measuring board · trash bag (drinking-water reservoir)

Placeholder slots: PFD · anchor · dry bag · light

Crappie

  • Light spinning 4–6 lb · 1/16–1/8 oz jigs · bobbers
  • Ruler for Hoover 9″ minimum (verify HuntFish OH)
  • Small net · minnow bucket if using live bait legally

Placeholder slots: jig pack · light combo · ruler

Bass

  • Medium spinning/baitcast 8–14 lb
  • Soft plastics · spinnerbait · topwater for low light
  • Terminal kit · pliers · 12″ measure (black bass min)

Placeholder slots: plastics bundle · topwater · pliers

Saugeye / walleye

  • Medium spinning · paddle-tails / twisters · crankbaits
  • Headlamp for night unhooking · long-nose pliers
  • Remember daily 6 combined walleye/sauger/saugeye — verify size on HuntFish OH

Placeholder slots: soft plastics · crankbait · headlamp

How to play

A quiet walk, then a cast

Open the vale from the Play page. Everything saves on this device. No account, no timers that lock fishing.

Play now

Controls

WASD or arrowsWalk. Packs up a waiting line (not a live minigame).
E or SpaceFish, talk, pick bait, sleep, craft, and play the hook minigame.
JJournal. Pauses the vale. Cancels a waiting bite; fails a live minigame.
IPack — rod, bait, coins, mute, copy/paste save.
1–5Equip bait while the pack is open.
EscClose menus or pack up the rod.

Fishing

  1. Stand on a dock or bank until the prompt reads Fish · Space or E.
  2. A cast spends one bait. Wrong bait means fewer bites, not a hard stop. Glow is strongly preferred in the cave.
  3. Watch the bobber. A real bite starts a minigame: pond and millpond use a wide timing bar; river is faster; lake is hold-to-tension; cave is an erratic band.
  4. Landed fish go in the journal. Misses still count as sightings (silhouette).

Where to fish

  • Calm Pond — west pier. Sit the dock to skip toward dusk or dawn.
  • Rocky River — north bridge. Crickets help.
  • Deep Misty Lake — east dock. Night eels after dark.
  • Crystal Cave Pool — south hill mouth. Glow bait, erratic tension.
  • Misty Millpond — east raft, after 10 unique fish, a cottage visit, and two hearts with a neighbor.

Cottage and neighbors

The cottage sits west of the crossroads. Inside: bed (sleep), mail, aquarium, trophy wall, packing bench, forecast board. Wren, Bramble, and Lark remember your last catch. One gift a day. Wren’s stall sells bait and later rods (River Finch, Cave Spine) for coins plus a specific fish.

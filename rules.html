Rule changes, March 1, 2027

The Ohio Wildlife Council voted on Oct. 28, 2026 to drop most lake by lake bass rules and Hoover’s crappie rule. Here is every water on this site that changes, what the rule is today and what it becomes.

Until March 1, 2027 the 2026–27 rules stand. Source: ODNR news release (Oct. 29, 2026).

Bass: to the statewide rule

Largemouth, smallmouth and spotted bass at these waters go to Ohio’s statewide rule: 5 a day, singly or in combination, 12″ minimum.

ODNR says most waters with their own bass rules move to the statewide limit, so other Ohio waters change too. Only the waters on this site are listed here.

Crappie: Hoover only

Hoover Reservoir today: 30 a day, black and white combined, 9″ minimum. From March 1, 2027: no daily limit and no minimum length, the statewide crappie rule.

Every other 30 a day, 9″ crappie water keeps its rule, Paint Creek Lake, Salt Fork Lake, Seneca Lake and the Portage Lakes included.

What this site is

  • A night-dock atmosphere and fishing journal look — visitors do not play a game here.
  • Sample log entries clearly marked SAMPLE so they are never confused with live bite reports.
  • Local-only trip storage (your device). No accounts. No backend.
  • Not ODNR. Not City of Columbus. Confirm licenses, limits, and closures yourself.
Night fishing can be dangerous in storms, heat, and low visibility. Wear a life jacket when the situation calls for it. Ohio fishing license required for ages 16+. See About for catfish limit reminders.

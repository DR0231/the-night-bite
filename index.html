How to read this
  • Pool is lake surface elevation in feet NGVD29 — not the depth of any hole or channel.
  • The ODNR contour map is drawn at 894 ft. If pool is lower, real water is shallower than printed contours by about (894 − pool).
  • Temp is gauge water temperature (sensor ~2 ft down) — not air temp, and not every creek arm.
  • USGS values here are provisional and can be revised later.
  • Hoover is a 10 HP / idle-speed drinking-water reservoir — keep wakes down and pack out trash.

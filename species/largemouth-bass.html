Species · Largemouth bass

Largemouth bass

More abundant than smallmouth on Hoover — docks, weeds, and shoreline cover (public water).

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: pre-spawn and spawn in shallows — be mindful of bed ethics and regs.
  • Summer: early/late and night around cover; midday deeper or shade.
  • Fall: chase bait schools and creek arms.
  • Winter: slow; deeper structure.

Time of day

Dawn, dusk, and night around lighted public structure. Midday summer often needs shade or deeper edges.

Where on Hoover

  • Public shoreline cover, weed edges, and ramp areas (Oxbow, Redbank, etc.).
  • Creek arms and quieter banks.
  • Boat: docks and points that are public — no casting onto private docks/yards.

Gear

Sized for bank reality and ≤10 hp boats — Hoover is a drinking-water reservoir.

  • Medium spinning or baitcasting, 8–14 lb line (fluoro or braid+leader).
  • Bank-friendly: soft plastics, spinnerbaits, topwater for low light.
  • 10 hp boat: same kits — keep a small selection, not a full tournament arsenal.

Bait & presentation

  • Soft plastics (worms, creature baits), spinnerbaits, crankbaits, topwater at night.
  • Bank: parallel cast along weed/rock edges.
  • Boat: docks, points, and channel swings.

Notes

ODNR Hoover page: largemouth more abundant than smallmouth. Aggregate limit with smallmouth/spotted.

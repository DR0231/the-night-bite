Black crappie

Solid Hoover panfish: spring banks and brush, then deeper wood and channel edges.

Alum Creek's coves north of Cheshire Road are the spring crappie spot.

Buckeye Lake warms first in central Ohio, so spring crappie come early and shallow.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: shallow banks and cover, peak bank fishing.
  • Summer: deeper brush, docks (public), channel swings.
  • Fall: follow bait into creeks and arms.
  • Winter: deeper schools, so vertical presentations from a boat.

Time of day

Dawn/dusk excellent. Night under lights (public) can be fun in summer. Midday summer often deeper.

Limits reminder, verify

Hoover has its own crappie rule in the 2026–27 booklet: 30 a day, 9″ minimum.

RuleReminder (verify 2026–27)
Minimum length at Hoover9″ (Hoover site-specific rule)
Daily bag30, black and white crappie combined (Hoover site-specific rule)

Coming March 1, 2027: the Ohio Wildlife Council voted on Oct. 28, 2026 to remove Hoover’s crappie daily limit and 9″ minimum. From that date Hoover follows the statewide crappie rule, no daily limit and no minimum size (ODNR news release). Until then, 30 a day and 9″ is the rule in force.

Hoover isn’t on ODNR’s list of 30 fish, 9″ crappie waters (2026–27 rules), so there is no daily limit or minimum size.

Species · White bass

White bass

Confirmed on the ODNR Hoover page — spring run up Big Walnut Creek and summer schools chasing shad.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: run up Big Walnut Creek (public access only — respect closures/property).
  • Summer: open-water schools on shad — watch birds and surface activity.
  • Fall: continue schooling until cool-down.
  • Winter: deeper, less of a night focus.

Time of day

Dawn/dusk and whenever shad are busting. Night can produce under lights or moon when schools are up.

Where on Hoover

  • Big Walnut Creek spring run — public banks/bridges only.
  • Main-lake points and open water when schooling.
  • Boat helps find schools; bank anglers watch windward points.

Gear

Sized for bank reality and ≤10 hp boats — Hoover is a drinking-water reservoir.

  • Medium-light spinning, small spoons, jigs, small swimbaits.
  • Boat: follow birds / graph; bank: cast to breaking fish.

Bait & presentation

  • White jigs, small spoons, inline spinners, soft jerkbaits.
  • Match shad size — downsize when picky.

Notes

ODNR Hoover: white bass spring run up Big Walnut Creek; schools chasing shad. Aggregate limit with hybrid/striped.

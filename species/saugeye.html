Species · Saugeye

Saugeye

Stocked saugeye rebounded since ~2017 — a real Hoover target for low-light trolling and casting.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: shallow low-light bites as water warms.
  • Summer: dawn/dusk and night; deeper by day.
  • Fall: strong feed along breaks.
  • Winter: slower, deeper presentations.

Time of day

Night and twilight are strengths — soft plastics and crankbaits along breaks. Daytime possible in stained water or deep.

Where on Hoover

  • Channel edges and breaks on the ODNR 894 map (reference).
  • Bank: cast along drops near Walnut / public riprap where safe.
  • Channel edges and breaks on the ODNR 894 map (reference).
  • Points near public ramps; lower lake structure by boat.

Gear

Sized for bank reality and ≤10 hp boats — Hoover is a drinking-water reservoir.

  • Medium spinning/baitcasting, 8–12 lb.
  • Headlamp for night unhooking.
  • Medium spinning/baitcasting, 8–12 lb.

  • Boat (≤10 hp): slow troll or cast breaks; bank: long casts along drop.

Bait & presentation

  • Twister tails, paddle-tails, crankbaits, jerkbaits in low light.
  • Night: darker or glow trailers; keep retrieves steady.

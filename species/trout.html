Species · Trout

Trout

On this site trout are stocked fish only. ODNR’s Fish Stocking dashboard lists rainbow trout at six of these waters in the last ten years, and brown trout once at Punderson Lake (2016) and once at Wolf Run Lake (2024).

Limits

Statewide: 5 trout a day, singly or in combination, no minimum length.

Some streams have their own trout rules, and brook trout may not be kept from the Ashtabula, Chagrin, Grand and Conneaut rivers and their tributaries. None of those streams is a water on this site.

Reminder only. Confirm on HuntFish OH / Ohio Fishing Regulations 2026–27 (PDF).

Winter drawdowns

Every winter the Corps of Engineers lowers the Muskingum lakes to make room for snowmelt and spring rain. Here is MWCD’s schedule for the waters on this site, so you know how far a ramp end might drop before you back down.

MWCD lakes, 2026–27

MWCD says the drawdown starts in early November and levels should be back to normal by February, with a suggested partial refill by March 15, 2027. Each lake page shows its own gauge, so check today’s level there.

State park lakes

ODNR keeps its own winter drawdown list for state park lakes. The Dock season notes on the Water page quote it for each water it covers, and say when it still shows last winter’s dates.

ODNR winter drawdowns Dock season

A lower lake means shallow ramp ends, new stumps and mud flats near the bank. Go slow at night and check the ramp before you launch.

About & disclaimer

The Night Bite is a personal, unofficial site — a night-fishing notebook and vegan snack page for Hoover Reservoir (Delaware / Franklin County, Ohio).

Not official

  • Not the Ohio Department of Natural Resources (ODNR).
  • Not the City of Columbus.
  • Not USGS. Gauge IDs and full-pool notes are for reference; live numbers on Water are a static snapshot (optionally refreshed from public USGS JSON) — never invented.
  • Depth contours linked from Water are ODNR 2001 / 894-ft pool — reference only, not for navigation.
  • Sample log trips are fiction for layout demos, stamped SAMPLE.

Safety

Fishing at night can be dangerous. Storms, lightning, heat, cold, slick ramps, and low visibility are real risks. Turn back when weather turns. Use lights, tell someone your plan, and wear a life jacket when appropriate.

License

An Ohio fishing license is required for anglers 16 and older (confirm current rules before you go). Buy and manage licenses through official ODNR / HuntFish OH channels — not through this site.

Statewide catfish limits (reminder only)

The following is a plain-language reminder of commonly published statewide daily limits for trophy-size cats. Regulations change. Confirm on HuntFish OH (or the current ODNR fishing regulations) before you keep fish.

  • Blue catfish: 1 fish ≥ 35 inches
  • Channel catfish: 1 fish ≥ 28 inches
  • Flathead catfish: 1 fish ≥ 35 inches

Smaller fish and other bag rules may also apply. This site does not replace the regulation booklet.

Privacy on The Log

Trip entries you add are stored in your browser’s localStorage only (key nightbite-log-v1). No accounts, no server database. Do not type secrets, card numbers, SSNs, or home addresses into the notes field.

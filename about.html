About & disclaimer

The Night Bite is a personal, unofficial Hoover Reservoir fishing hub with a night-dock look — species pages, water maps, trip log, gear, and vegan dockside snacks (Delaware / Franklin County, Ohio). Night is especially useful for channel and flathead cats (blues too); it is not the best approach for every species.

Not official

  • Not the Ohio Department of Natural Resources (ODNR).
  • Not the City of Columbus.
  • Not USGS. Gauge snapshots are provisional and labeled; optional refresh never invents numbers.
  • Depth contours linked from Water are ODNR 2001 / 894-ft pool — reference only, not for navigation. No Garmin/Navionics.
  • Sample log trips are fiction for layout demos, stamped SAMPLE.
  • Tournaments listed only when submitted — this site is not a tournament director.

Safety

Fishing after dark can be dangerous. Storms, lightning, heat, cold, slick ramps, and low visibility are real risks. Turn back when weather turns. Use lights, tell someone your plan, and wear a life jacket when appropriate. Hoover is a drinking-water reservoir — pack out trash, follow posted rules.

License

An Ohio fishing license is required for anglers 16 and older (confirm current rules before you go). Buy and manage licenses through official ODNR / HuntFish OH channels — not through this site.

ODNR licenses / HuntFish OH · mention the HuntFish OH app for phone-side regs checks.

Regs reminders (verify 2026–27)

Plain-language reminders from the statewide inland pattern in ODNR Fishing Regulations 2026–27 (verify current). Confirm on HuntFish OH before you keep fish. Some waters differ (e.g. Hoover crappie 9″ note).

  • Channel cat: under 28″ no daily limit; 28″+ daily 1
  • Blue cat: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Flathead: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Largemouth / smallmouth / spotted: 5 combined, 12″ min
  • Striped / hybrid striped / white bass: 30 combined; no more than 4 over 15″
  • Walleye / sauger / saugeye: 6 combined; statewide table min “None” — some lakes differ; verify HuntFish for Hoover
  • Crappie at Hoover: 9″ minimum (ODNR Hoover Fishing Area page) — verify HuntFish OH 2026; daily often 30 black+white combined statewide
  • Bluegill / sunfish, carp, gar: verify HuntFish OH rather than inventing bags here

Privacy on The Log

Personal trip entries you add are stored in your browser’s localStorage only (key nightbite-log-v1). Community submissions (Phase 1) use mailto or GitHub issues — no accounts required to read the static JSON list. Do not type secrets, card numbers, SSNs, or home addresses into notes.
